COVER-THE-WHOLE-MARKET — what is in this zip, 2026-09-30
=======================================================

START HERE
  python main.py selftest                       # 104 maths + 32 market tests, offline
  pip install -r requirements.txt               # needed for anything live

WHAT THIS IS
  The DEX price-fetcher from before, plus the answer to "automate all coins
  listed on PancakeSwap and Uniswap, not just one or two":

    dex/multicall.py      batched chain reads (6,000 calls per request)
    dex/market_index.py   a local index of every liquid pool, and the two ways
                          to fill it (walk the factory / ask it about a list)
    tests/test_market.py  32 offline tests, most of them about scale
    docs/market-index.md  the design, the measurements, and the honest limits

  Nothing in any of it signs or sends a transaction. A test asserts that the
  read path contains no send/sign/key call at all.

TRY IT IN FIVE MINUTES
  python main.py arb index discover --network bsc --token-list tokens.json
  python main.py arb index stats --network bsc
  python main.py arb market --network bsc --pairs 5 --refresh

  (state/market_index.sqlite is already in this zip, filled with 10,408 pools
   across Pancake V2, Pancake V3 and Uniswap V3, so the market scan works
   immediately without rebuilding anything.)

NUMBERS WORTH KNOWING
  * one read at a time            5 reads/s
  * one Multicall3 request        6,000 calls in ~0.65 s -> ~10,000 reads/s
  * the same request, 6 hours later, same endpoint: 12 s. Public RPCs throttle.
    The index is therefore resumable and reports coverage as a number.
  * PancakeSwap V2 has 3,228,724 pairs. Newest 6,000: 1.9% had real liquidity.
    Oldest 6,000: 29%. The deep pairs are old — the top of the factory is noise.

TWO WAYS TO COVER A TOKEN LIST
  * `arb index discover --token-list <json>`  seconds.   Asks the factories
    "does this pair exist?" for every token against every anchor. Covers a
    published tokenlist completely, finds 4x more pools than an hour of walking.
  * `arb index build`  hours.  Walks the factory and discovers tokens nobody
    told us about — the only way to reach a long-tail pair with no anchor side.
    Resumable, newest- or oldest-first, safe to interrupt.
